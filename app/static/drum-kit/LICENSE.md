## Studio multisample kit

Files under `studio/` are a curated browser-ready subset of the **FreePats
General MIDI percussion set**, version 2020-08-22. They provide multiple
velocity and randomized layers for the acoustic kit used by Web MIDI.

- Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/SoundSets/general-midi.html
- Original archive: `FreePatsGM-Percussion-SFZ-20200822.tar.xz`
